A good presentation system helps with three things

The hard part is not making a nice slide; it is rebuilding every time.

  • 1. Unified design language

    All pages share tokens, so type, color, and shadow stay consistent.

  • 2. Lower reuse cost

    For the next similar talk, copy the deck, change data, and switch theme.

  • 3. Switchable and demo-ready

    Press T to cycle themes and A to change animations live.
